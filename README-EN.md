## Why This Collection

- **13 cities in verse** — Hangzhou, Beijing, Nanjing, Suzhou, Xi'an, Luoyang, and more
- **11 classical *ci* forms** — Magpie Bridge, Butterfly in Love with Flowers, Prelude to Water Melody…
- **Illustrated with photography** — 32 location photographs paired with poems
- **Scholarly commentary** — each poem includes deep analysis of allusions, imagery, and philosophy
- **A living e-book** — open `index.html`, and the poems appear in vertical script on a rice-paper canvas

## About the Poet

**Wei Dongpo** writes from Hangzhou, China. His pen name pays homage to Su Dongpo (Su Shi), the great Song Dynasty poet. Working across classical forms and modern verse, he brings Chinese aesthetics to a global audience.

> *"Life passes as swiftly as a white colt glimpsed through a crack in the wall. Wei Dongpo was born in this era, and will leave his mark — proof that he, too, once walked this world."*
